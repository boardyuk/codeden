- **The orchestrator and project managers switch provider when theirs runs out.** When their provider hits its usage limit mid-turn, CodeDen moves them to another provider that is installed and signed in, sends your message again, and says so in their chat and the inbox: "Switched from A to B: A hit its usage limit (until 14:30)". Notes, wake-ups, inbox items and queued messages carry over. Once the limit has reset, their next turn moves back to the provider you chose. If no other provider is ready, nothing is switched and the chat says why. Turn it off with **Switch provider automatically when a limit is hit** in **Settings → Providers → Defaults per role**.
